# Tradebilia Homepage Reference Notes

The public Wix homepage confirms the intended composition more clearly than the earlier static screenshot. The homepage uses a very slim black utility bar with the word **Search** at left, a short white search input with a purple search button, and compact right-aligned account/action text and icons.

The category strip is shallow, bright, and tightly packed. It presents a compact row of tabs with dark text on a pale background rather than a tall navigation block.

The hero banner is **shorter and flatter** than the current implementation in the sandbox. The oversized Tradebilia logo is centered on a deep blue banner, but the banner height is restrained enough that the silver metrics strip is visible immediately below it on a typical desktop viewport.

The silver metrics strip is a thin horizontal band with four equal metrics: Total Members, Total Items, Total Value, and Total Trades. The labels are small and understated, while the values are bold and prominent.

Below the metrics strip, the content is arranged in a left-and-right layout. A narrow purple quick-links rail sits on the left, while the main area on the right shows a simple white Recently Added shelf with five product cards.

The lower section uses four full-height adjacent ranking panels: Most Viewed, Most Requested, Top Rated Traders, and Highest Trade Value. These panels have distinct backgrounds, minimal borders, strong headings, and compact ranked text lists.
