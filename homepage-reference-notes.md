# Tradebilia Homepage Reference Notes

The public Wix homepage confirms the intended composition more clearly than the earlier static screenshot. The homepage uses a very slim black utility bar with the word **Search** at left, a short white search input with a purple search button, and compact right-aligned account/action text and icons.

The category strip is shallow, bright, and tightly packed. It presents a compact row of tabs with dark text on a pale background rather than a tall navigation block.

The hero banner is **shorter and flatter** than the current implementation in the sandbox. The oversized Tradebilia logo is centered on a deep blue banner, but the banner height is restrained enough that the silver metrics strip is visible immediately below it on a typical desktop viewport.

The silver metrics strip is a thin horizontal band with four equal metrics: Total Members, Total Items, Total Value, and Total Trades. The labels are small and understated, while the values are bold and prominent.

Below the metrics strip, the content is arranged in a left-and-right layout. A narrow purple quick-links rail sits on the left, while the main area on the right shows a simple white Recently Added shelf with five product cards.

The lower section uses four full-height adjacent ranking panels: Most Viewed, Most Requested, Top Rated Traders, and Highest Trade Value. These panels have distinct backgrounds, minimal borders, strong headings, and compact ranked text lists.

## Exact-copy comparison refresh

The public Wix homepage uses a very bold oversized **Search** wordmark at the far left of a black header strip, followed by a compact search input and purple search button. The category strip beneath it uses large condensed labels in evenly sized white tabs with thin separators. The hero banner is a full-width navy image with the logo scaled smaller relative to the banner height than in the current implementation. The silver metrics band uses compact labels and very large black numbers. The left-edge quick-links column begins immediately below the metrics band and continues visually as the first column of the next horizontal content band. The **Recently Added** heading is significantly larger and more serif-forward than the current implementation, and the product cards begin directly to the right of the left-edge column.

## Current preview gap check

The latest preview is closer, but it still differs from the Wix homepage in several visible ways. The hero logo is still too large relative to the banner height, so the banner feels more dominant than the Wix version. The metrics numbers are closer, but the top header account area remains smaller and lighter than the Wix reference. The left quick-links rail now exists, yet the overall page still needs the left-edge treatment to feel more continuous and structural across the content bands. The next pass should reduce the hero logo scale, tighten the header and category spacing further, and strengthen the left-side column continuity so the first viewport reads more like the Wix homepage.
