# Homepage Social Section Visual Check

The development homepage was captured at desktop width (1280px) and phone width (390px). The new red YouTube icon appears alongside Facebook, Instagram, and X in the Collector Community section. The desktop social pills remain aligned in one row. At phone width, the pills wrap within the section without clipping or overlap. Because no official Tradebilia YouTube channel URL was provided, YouTube is intentionally rendered as a clearly labeled non-clickable “coming soon” pill rather than pointing to an invented or generic destination.
