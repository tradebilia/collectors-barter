## Asset and Composition

The global Search Results hero uses the generated, wide all-category collectible collage at `/manus-storage/tradebilia-global-search-collage-hero_7a52c84a.png`. It combines recognizable visual references to the marketplace’s collection types—cards, comics, coins, games, stamps, toys, film, autographs, and pins—without adding title text or replacing the existing animated Tradebilia SVG.

The page overlays the collage with a dark teal-to-navy gradient and retains a centered low-detail safe area for `AnimatedLogoSmall70`. The hero is now 400 pixels minimum height, matching the Category Page hero proportion, and the existing Category Bar remains immediately below it.
