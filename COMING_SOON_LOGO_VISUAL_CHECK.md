# Coming Soon Logo Visual Check

The Coming Soon SVG logo was reviewed at 1280x720 and 390x844 after the logo-only scale increase.

At desktop width, the enlarged logo has a more pronounced appearance, remains centered within the parchment composition, and does not clip against the frame or page edges. The tagline and the rest of the desktop composition remain visible below it.

At phone width, the larger logo remains horizontally contained and centered within its existing overflow-hidden frame. The phone layout continues to stack the tagline, highlights, launching stamp, categories, and signup controls without a logo-induced horizontal overflow. The responsive background composition naturally places the upper logo area over the darker workbench region at this viewport; no background, hero, or non-logo styles were changed for this request.
