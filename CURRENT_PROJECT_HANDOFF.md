

## Title-derived evidence participates in universal ranking — November 7, 2026
Clarified and corrected the ranking path for incomplete marketplace metadata. The title-derived comparison column was previously diagnostic evidence only; recognized title evidence was not uniformly marked as the source of ranking points. Universal ranking now treats structured candidate Grade or certification-company values of `Unknown`, `N/A`, or equivalent empty states as missing and uses the centralized recognized title parser instead. A title such as `PSA 9.8` can therefore contribute the matching numeric-grade points and grading-company points when the candidate provider fields are unknown. Structured non-unknown candidate values remain authoritative, and arbitrary title text is not promoted into ranking metadata.

The ranking explanation records the source explicitly as `grade (title-derived)` and/or `grading company (title-derived)`.
