## Video Games ranking and visual caps — October 30, 2026
Added source-neutral marketplace/eBay ranking for the three comparable Video Games item types. **Game** uses a 100-point score: game title 30, platform 20, release year 15, region 10, edition 10, product form 10, and UPC/EAN 5. **Console** uses: console name 30, model number 20, region 15, working condition 15, original box 10, cables 5, and controllers 5. **Accessory** uses: accessory type 25, accessory name 25, compatible platform 20, manufacturer 10, working condition 10, and original packaging 10.

Collection/Lot does not receive these scores or caps and remains blocked from provider lookups, visual analysis, and trade analysis. ## Movies / Individual Movie, Box Set, and Collection/Lot — October 30, 2026
