# Tradebilia Coming Soon Redesign Directions

The current page is composed and restrained, but it reads as a generic polished waitlist rather than a specific collectors trading exchange. The next version should retain the calm hierarchy and email capture while giving the page one unmistakable collector-native motif.

## Direction 1 — Auction Catalogue

An ivory editorial catalogue with fine archival rules, specimen labels, lot-number details, and a cobalt action accent. The tone is premium, quiet, and trustworthy. It treats the waitlist as an invitation to a curated exchange rather than a standard product launch.

**Best for:** a classic, timeless collector identity with broad cross-category appeal.

## Direction 2 — Private Exchange Ledger

A deep ink-and-navy invitation card with engraved details and one electric-blue rule. It feels like membership in a serious private exchange, with category line art framing the waitlist rather than distracting from it.

**Best for:** a more exclusive, secure, high-trust positioning.

## Direction 3 — Restoration Workbench

A warm, tactile archival worktable with carefully composed collectible artifacts at the edges and a clean central signup panel. This has the strongest immediate collector-world storytelling but needs disciplined restraint to avoid looking like a collage.

**Best for:** the richest visual connection to all ten categories.

## Direction 4 — Collection Registry

A crisp gallery-white page with an asymmetric cobalt column, museum-label details, and a precise editorial grid. It is contemporary, intentional, and significantly less generic than the existing centered template.

**Best for:** a modern, design-led collector brand that still feels archival and trusted.

## Guardrails for Every Direction

The selected direction should preserve the existing working signup behavior and include all ten categories through restrained motifs. It should not show fabricated reviews, fake user activity, or public launch claims. Any displayed email capture language should remain consent-based. The published page remains unchanged until a direction is selected.
