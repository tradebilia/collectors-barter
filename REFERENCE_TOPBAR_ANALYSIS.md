# Prior-Session Top-Bar Reference Analysis

## Crops 1–2

The reference uses a **compact 56-pixel black top bar**. Its animated mark begins near the left edge and consists of a small multicolor wheel followed by a thin white divider and a single-line wordmark. The fixed `TRADE` prefix is white and the changing category suffix is color-coded. The wheel in this navigation mark is substantially smaller than the separate, large wheel shown in the homepage hero.

The prior-session hero begins immediately below this compact bar. The large hero wheel and title must therefore remain independent from the compact navigation implementation.

## Crops 3–4

The reference confirms that the compact black bar remains a single horizontal strip across the entire viewport, with the search centered and sign-in aligned at the far right. The hero content starts below the bar and the category navigation stays directly below the hero. The compact navigation mark should therefore use a small fixed-width logo area rather than the wide 650-pixel desktop treatment currently used by the shared top bar.
