# Member Directory visual verification

The `/members` route was visually checked at desktop and mobile widths after the verification refinement. Administrator displays all connected account badges—eBay, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Etsy—in the Account verifications section. A member with merchant approval displays a separate green Verified Merchant badge beside the display name and does not have it repeated in the account-verification list. Members without connected accounts show “No account verifications.”

At 390px wide, the merchant badge wraps cleanly beside the member name, account-verification badges remain readable and contained within the card, and no horizontal overflow was observed. The existing merchant-only filter remains separate in the Trust & experience panel.
