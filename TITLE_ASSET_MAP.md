# Supplied Page Title Asset Map

The supplied title assets are mapped to the existing page heroes as follows: Account Settings, Account Setup, Add to Your Inventory, Collector's Forum, Community Trade Evaluation, Contact Us, Conventions, Messages, Most Viewed, Most Requested, Highest Trade Value, My Inventory, Profile, Referral Request, Report a User, Top Rated Traders, Trade Hub, Traders Showcase, Verified Merchants, Watchlist, and the shared Tradebilia header logo.

The current application has **no standalone routes or hero containers** for `Inbox.svg`, `TradeRequest.svg`, `TradeHistory.svg`, or `RatingsandReview.svg`. These source files are retained in persistent project asset storage and are intentionally not injected into an unrelated page. They should be applied when the corresponding standalone Inbox, Trade Request, Trade History, or Ratings and Review routes are introduced.
